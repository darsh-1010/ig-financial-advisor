Can I get a car loan, and how much?

Yes, we can help you apply. The amount you can borrow depends on the lender, your income, your credit score and the car you choose. We'll tell you what's realistic before you apply.

How long does car loan approval take?

It depends on the lender and how quickly your documents are verified. Complete, correct documents are the best way to speed it up, and we'll keep you updated at every step.

What documents are required for a car loan?

Usually identity and address proof (Aadhaar and PAN), income proof such as salary slips or ITR, recent bank statements, and the car's quotation. The exact list can vary by lender.

Can I get a car loan with a low credit score?

Often yes, but the interest rate may be higher and the lender may ask for a larger down payment. A quick chat with us will show which options are open to you.

Are there any hidden charges?

Lenders charge things like processing fees and may charge for foreclosure or part-payment. We explain every applicable charge upfront, so read the loan terms and ask us about anything unclear.
